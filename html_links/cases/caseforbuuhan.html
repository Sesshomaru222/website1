The Case For Buuhan

In contrast to my case for Kid Buu, here I will present the case that most fans find more compelling: that Buuhan is the strongest form of Majin Buu. Just about every popular influencer in the community supports this claim, and there are several strong arguments that they use to back it up. I will lay them out plainly and objectively here, but like I said in the case for Kid Buu, this doesn't necessarily reflect my personal opinion. I will flag points that have been refuted as flat out false, misleading, or debunked by canon material in order to provide a balanced analysis.

Common Sense
Many argue that it's only natural for Buuhan to be the strongest form of Buu, given that he is a fusion of Super Buu and Ultimate Gohan, both of whom are incredibly powerful in their own right. The fusion combines the strengths of both fighters, resulting in a being that is exponentially stronger than either of them individually. This argument is based on the established mechanics of fusion in the Dragon Ball universe, where fused characters typically exhibit power levels that are significantly higher than their individual counterparts. Buu's feats as Buuhan, such as easily overpowering Vegeta and Goku, further support this argument.
