The Case For Kid Buu

In the Dragon Ball community, Kid Buu is the subject of much debate. Many fans believe the strongest form of Majin Buu is actually Buuhan, the fusion of Super Buu and Ultimate Gohan. Still others argue that Kid Buu is the most powerful form of Buu. In this case, I will lay out the arguments that support the idea that Kid Buu is indeed the strongest incarnation of Majin Buu. Please note that, this is not necessarily my personal opinion, but rather an exploration of the arguments in favor of Kid Buu's strength. I will also flag points that have been refuted as flat out false, misleading, or debunked by canon material in order to provide a balanced analysis.

Toriyama said so
When asked about the strength of Kid Buu, Akira Toriyama stated that Kid Buu was the strongest form of Buu. This is a direct statement from the creator himself, which supports the argument that Kid Buu is indeed the most powerful incarnation of Majin Buu.
