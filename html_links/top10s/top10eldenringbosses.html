Top 10 ELDEN RING Bosses

Elden Ring stands out among other games for its extraordinarily challenging and memorable bosses. There's no option to change the difficulty to scale down these bosses and Hidetaka Miyazaki makes no qualms about making your life hard, so players must rely on their skills and strategies to overcome them. Players who manage to overcome these bosses earn a unique prestige in the gaming community. Here, I want to layout the bosses that are particularly challenging and who win players the most bragging rights. So, here are my top 10 Elden Ring bosses.

  1. Radagon of the Golden Order -
  2. Godfrey, First Elden Lord - This fight felt right. The first elden Lord vs the next. There's a reason Godfrey was revered in his day, and why Radahn looked up to him. There aren't many things more terrifying than when he decides to cast off his persona and fight you as Horah Lu, Warrior. Wouldn't recommend. All things considered, he seems like a nice guy.
  3. Starscrourge Radahn - A monster. I can't help but appreciate his greatness while scrambling to get a hit in, wondering if I'm closing in at a bad time.. He is magnificent, and his lore drills that in even more.
  4. The Black Blade, Maliketh - What a crazy fight this was. Maliketh is quick and deadly, even among other boss fights.
  5. Bayle The Dread - This fight was was over-the-top dramatic it felt like you were fighting the final boss— I loved every second of it. The fight with Bayle was an incredible experience, and one that you need to come prepared for.
  6. Malenia, Blade of Miquella - The goddess of scarlet rot is a truly frustrating opponent. Perhaps the craziest thing about this fight is that she only uses a sword initially where others would hurl ungodly magical meteors at you (Radahn, I'm looking at you). This is a strong, independent woman if I've ever seen one. My advice: spend your runes first or lose them forever.
  7. Miquella & Radahn, The Promised Consort - Holy $#!^. If you have time to kill—about a year or so, then have at it. This fight will take patience, precision, and an enourmous amount of luck. If you're the type to throw your controller then may need a few extra controllers too. I will say, though, that these two are a glorious duo who have every right to rule the land between. In short, spend your runes first.
